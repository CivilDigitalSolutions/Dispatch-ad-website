What Dispatch does

Everything your business needs, in one place

Built around the day-to-day of a trades business — electricians, plumbers, HVAC and beyond — so your office and your field team spend less time on paperwork and more time on the job.

Structured fault reports

Every report follows your own template — the right questions, every time — so nothing gets missed between the customer and the technician on site.

Secure customer portal links

Send customers a single secure link — no account, no app. They describe the fault, answer your questionnaire and attach photos straight from their phone.

Job dispatch — List, Kanban & Map

Assign jobs to the right person on your team and track them however suits you: a working list, a drag-and-drop Kanban board, or every open job plotted on a map, with in-app notifications keeping everyone in sync.

Invoicing, start to finish

Raise an invoice from the job, issue it as a branded PDF and email it to the customer — then track drafts, sent, overdue and paid invoices from the job or across the whole business, right through to credit notes when something needs correcting.

Get paid by card

Customers can pay their invoice by card online from a secure link — no chasing a bank transfer. Take part payments and deposits, and record any payment, by card or otherwise, against the invoice as it comes in.

Quotes your customer can accept online

Price a job up on the doorstep, send it as a branded PDF, and the customer accepts or declines from a link — no phone tag. Ask for a deposit as a percentage of the quote, and accepting raises the deposit invoice for you, ready to pay by card.

Week calendar & scheduling

Book visits onto a week calendar and see at a glance who is where. Each card carries the job reference, the time, and the town and postcode. Clashes warn rather than block — a ten-minute call between two jobs is normal working — and the customer gets a confirmation when you book, move or cancel.

Automatic payment reminders

Switch reminders on and an unpaid invoice chases itself — three emails at 3, 10 and 21 days past due, in your name, then it stops and leaves you to it. Off by default, off per invoice whenever you want, and never sent for an invoice you haven't emailed.

Digital records for Making Tax Digital

Photograph a receipt on site and record what it cost. Dispatch keeps your income and expenses as digital records and exports them via a digital link into your own Making Tax Digital software — it never files anything with HMRC, and what you submit stays yours to check.

Reusable templates & questionnaires

Build a template once — text, numbers, photos, signatures and more — and reuse it across every customer report of that type, so quality doesn't depend on who's asking the questions.

Team roles, permissions & invites

Owner, office admin and field-team roles give each person exactly the access they need. Invite teammates with a shareable link, seat-limited by your plan. See the full permissions table →

Media capture, attached to the job

Photos, videos and files from the customer's report and from the technician on site sit together on the same job — a complete record without a single email attachment.

How it works

Three steps from fault to fixed

1

Report

The customer opens a secure portal link, answers your template's questions and attaches photos — no app, no account needed.

2

Dispatch

The report becomes a job. Quote for it if the customer needs a price first, then assign it, book it onto the week calendar and track it on the List, Kanban board or Map — however your business works.

3

Resolve

Your technician updates status and adds their own notes, photos and costs as the job progresses — then invoice the customer and get paid by card, without leaving Dispatch.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Dispatch available yet?

Yes — Dispatch is live. Start here and your business can be raising and dispatching fault reports today.

Does Dispatch work without an internet connection?

Dispatch is an online, browser-based application by design — there's no offline mode. Your team and customers need an internet connection to submit fault reports, view jobs or use the map, which keeps every report, photo and job update in sync across your business in real time.

What does Dispatch cost?

Dispatch has a free tier plus three paid tiers priced by number of seats and templates — see current plans and pricing above, or get in touch and we'll talk you through it.

Is Dispatch built for a specific trade?

No — Dispatch works for any field-service trade: electricians, plumbers, HVAC engineers and more. It's built around structured fault reports, job dispatch and reusable templates that adapt to how your business works, and it's operated by Civil Digital.

Can I invoice customers and get paid online?

Yes. Raise an invoice from the job, send it as a branded PDF by email, and your customer can pay by card securely online, right from the invoice. Part payments and deposits are supported, and you can switch on automatic reminders for anything that goes past due. See our Terms for details on payment processing.

Is there a scheduling calendar?

Yes. Book visits onto a week calendar, see who is booked in where, and let the customer know automatically when a visit is booked, moved or cancelled. It sits alongside the List, Kanban and Map views. Dispatch's calendar does not sync with Google Calendar, Outlook or iCal.

Can I send quotes as well as invoices?

Yes. Price a job up, send the quote as a branded PDF, and the customer accepts or declines it from a link — you see the answer in Dispatch. You can ask for a deposit as a percentage of the quote; when the customer accepts, Dispatch raises the deposit invoice so they can pay it by card straight away, and drafts the final invoice ready for when the work is done.

Does Dispatch handle Making Tax Digital?

Dispatch keeps your income and expenses as digital records — your technicians can photograph a receipt on site — and exports them via a digital link into your own Making Tax Digital software. Dispatch does not file anything with HMRC and is not a substitute for your accountant or your filing software; what gets submitted stays your responsibility.

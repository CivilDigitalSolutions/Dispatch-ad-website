What Dispatch does

Everything your business needs, in one place

Built around the day-to-day of a trades business — electricians, plumbers, HVAC and beyond — so your office and your field team spend less time on paperwork and more time on the job.

Structured fault reports

Every report follows your own template — the right questions, every time — so nothing gets missed between the customer and the technician on site.

Secure customer portal links

Send customers a single secure link — no account, no app. They describe the fault, answer your questionnaire and attach photos straight from their phone.

Job dispatch — List, Kanban & Map

Assign jobs to the right person on your team and track them however suits you: a working list, a drag-and-drop Kanban board, or every open job plotted on a map, with in-app notifications keeping everyone in sync.

Invoicing, start to finish

Raise an invoice from the job, issue it as a branded PDF and email it to the customer — then track drafts, sent, overdue and paid invoices from the job or across the whole business, right through to credit notes when something needs correcting.

Get paid by card

Customers can pay their invoice by card online from a secure link — no chasing a bank transfer. Record any payment, by card or otherwise, against the invoice as it comes in.

Reusable templates & questionnaires

Build a template once — text, numbers, photos, signatures and more — and reuse it across every customer report of that type, so quality doesn't depend on who's asking the questions.

Team roles, permissions & invites

Owner, office admin and field-team roles give each person exactly the access they need. Invite teammates with a shareable link, seat-limited by your plan. See the full permissions table →

Media capture, attached to the job

Photos, videos and files from the customer's report and from the technician on site sit together on the same job — a complete record without a single email attachment.

How it works

Three steps from fault to fixed

1

Report

The customer opens a secure portal link, answers your template's questions and attaches photos — no app, no account needed.

2

Dispatch

The report becomes a job. Assign it to a team member and track it on the List, Kanban board or Map, however your business works.

3

Resolve

Your technician updates status and adds their own notes and photos as the job progresses — then invoice the customer and get paid by card, without leaving Dispatch.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Dispatch available yet?

Yes — Dispatch is live. Start here and your business can be raising and dispatching fault reports today.

Does Dispatch work without an internet connection?

Dispatch is an online, browser-based application by design — there's no offline mode. Your team and customers need an internet connection to submit fault reports, view jobs or use the map, which keeps every report, photo and job update in sync across your business in real time.

What does Dispatch cost?

Dispatch has a free tier plus three paid tiers priced by number of seats and templates — see current plans and pricing above, or get in touch and we'll talk you through it.

Is Dispatch built for a specific trade?

No — Dispatch works for any field-service trade: electricians, plumbers, HVAC engineers and more. It's built around structured fault reports, job dispatch and reusable templates that adapt to how your business works, and it's operated by Civil Digital.

Can I invoice customers and get paid online?

Yes. Raise an invoice from the job, send it as a branded PDF by email, and your customer can pay by card securely online, right from the invoice. See our Terms for details on payment processing.

Is there a scheduling calendar?

Not yet — a calendar for planning jobs and appointments across your team is on the roadmap and coming soon. Today you can plan and track work with the List, Kanban and Map views.

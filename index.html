What Dispatch does

Everything your business needs, in one place

Built around the day-to-day of an electrical contractor, so your office and your electricians spend less time on paperwork and more time on the job.

Structured fault reports

Every report follows your own template — the right questions, every time — so nothing gets missed between the customer and the electrician on site.

Secure customer portal links

Send customers a single secure link — no account, no app. They describe the fault, answer your questionnaire and attach photos straight from their phone.

Job dispatch — List, Kanban & Map

Assign jobs to the right electrician and track them however suits you: a working list, a drag-and-drop Kanban board, or every open job plotted on a map, with in-app notifications keeping everyone in sync.

Reusable templates & questionnaires

Build a template once — text, numbers, photos, signatures and more — and reuse it across every customer report of that type, so quality doesn't depend on who's asking the questions.

Team roles & permissions

Owner, Office Admin and Electrician roles give each person exactly the access they need — from running the whole business to just working their own jobs.

Photo capture, attached to the job

Photos from the customer's report and from the electrician on site sit together on the same job — a complete visual record without a single email attachment.

How it works

Three steps from fault to fixed

1

Report

The customer opens a secure portal link, answers your template's questions and attaches photos — no app, no account needed.

2

Dispatch

The report becomes a job. Assign it to an electrician and track it on the List, Kanban board or Map, however your business works.

3

Resolve

The electrician updates status and adds their own notes and photos as the job progresses, so everyone always knows where it stands.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Dispatch available yet?

Yes — Dispatch is live. Start a free trial and your business can be raising and dispatching fault reports today.

Does Dispatch work without an internet connection?

Dispatch is an online, browser-based application by design — there's no offline mode. Your team and customers need an internet connection to submit fault reports, view jobs or use the map, which keeps every report, photo and job update in sync across your business in real time.

What does Dispatch cost?

Dispatch has a free tier plus three paid tiers priced by number of seats and templates — see current plans and pricing above, or get in touch and we'll talk you through it.

Is Dispatch built specifically for electricians?

Yes. Dispatch is built around the real workflow of UK electrical contractors — structured fault reports, job dispatch and reusable report templates — rather than being a generic field-service tool. It's operated by Civil Digital.
